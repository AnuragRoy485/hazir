The Hazir guide

Hosting, step by step.

Everything below takes minutes. Guests never need an account or an app - they open your link and answer.

1. Create your event

  1. Tap Start free and make your host account (email and a password).
  2. Tap New event. Give it a name, pick the occasion and date, and enter how many guests you are inviting.
  3. Optional but useful: venue, a welcome message, an RSVP deadline (with timezone), and an end time so the event archives itself.
  4. Choose which questions guests see: meal choice, arrival time and a note can each be asked or skipped.
New here? The empty dashboard has a Try a sample event button that pre-fills everything, so you can look around first.

2. Share one link

  • Open invite link - anyone with it can answer. Send it on WhatsApp or paste it anywhere.
  • QR code - download or share a branded QR image; guests scan it with their phone camera.
  • Personal links - add guest names and each person gets a private link, so you can see exactly who has not answered and send them a reminder in one tap.

3. Guests answer in seconds

Guests say yes or no, how many of them are coming (adults and kids), pick a meal, and set an expected arrival time. The page speaks English, Hindi and Bengali. After answering they can add the event to Google or Apple Calendar, and save their personal link to update the answer later - or delete it themselves.

4. Watch the headcount

Your dashboard shows confirmed, declined and awaiting counts, plates to prepare, meal breakdown, groups, arrivals by hour, and the latest answers - refreshing live. Filter and search the guest list, export it as CSV or Excel, or copy the one-tap Catering & RSVP Summary.

5. Weddings and multi-function events

When creating the event, add its functions (Sangeet, ceremony, reception...) - each with its own date and time. Guests still get one link, but they answer per function, and your dashboard (and the caterer view) shows separate counts and meal totals per function. The spreadsheet export gets one column per function.

6. Share a read-only caterer view

Every event has a Caterer / planner view link in the Invites tab. It is a live, read-only page with the headcount, plates, meals and arrival times - never guest names, phone numbers or emails. It refreshes itself every 30 seconds.

7. Extras hosts ask about

  • Co-hosts - add them by email in the Invites tab. They manage the event with you and stay invisible to guests and to each other.
  • Gift registry - paste a registry link when creating the event; guests see it as a small button on the invite. Hazir never handles payments.
  • Check-in - on the day, mark guests arrived in the Day of tab.
  • Banner - upload an event banner; it becomes the invite's preview image on WhatsApp and Facebook.
  • Add to Home Screen - Hazir is installable: open the menu in your phone browser and choose "Add to Home Screen".

8. Privacy in one paragraph

No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling data. Hosts can export or delete their events anytime; guests can update or delete their own answer from their saved RSVP link. The full policy is on the Privacy page.
