# Changelog

All notable changes to Hazir are recorded here, newest first.

## 2026-11-15 - Worldwide quick wins + bigger items

- RSVP page speaks **English, Hindi and Bengali** (guest picks, remembered on the device).
- **Add to calendar** after answering: Google Calendar link and an .ics download. The invite shows the event time with its timezone.
- **Multi-function events (wedding mode)**: one invite link, guests answer per function, per-function counts and meal totals on the dashboard, in the caterer view and in the spreadsheet export.
- **Caterer / planner view**: a read-only live summary link per event - headcount, plates, meals and arrivals, never guest names or contacts. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
- **Gift registry link** on events: a button on the invite. Link only, no payments.
- Guests can **delete their own answer** from their saved RSVP link.
- Worldwide occasions (Eid, Diwali, Christmas, Thanksgiving, graduations, housewarmings, reunions...) and meal choices (halal, kosher, gluten-free, nut allergy).
- **PWA**: installable to the home screen (manifest + icons).
- SEO: sitemap.xml, robots.txt with the sitemap line, JSON-LD SoftwareApplication, per-event link previews carry the event name with date and time.
- Accessibility: visible focus rings, 44px tap targets, labelled controls, reduced-motion honored.
- Performance: the ticker and animated counters pause while off screen.
- First-run checklist plus a **Try a sample event** button on the empty dashboard.
- New: public [guide](https://www.hazir.live/docs), this changelog, and CONTRIBUTING.md.
