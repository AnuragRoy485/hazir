The problem

Four hundred invited.
Everyone says yes.
Nobody tells you the time.

Hosts order food for a number they are guessing. Guests say "will try" and turn up two hours late, or not at all. The kitchen, the venue and the budget all pay for it.

Hazir replaces the guess with three plain answers from every guest: am I coming, how many of us, and when will we arrive. You see the real number as it forms.

How it works

Four steps. No spreadsheet.

  1. 01

    Create the event

    Name it, set the date and the number you invited. Choose the occasion and get one link.

  2. 02

    Share one link

    Send it on WhatsApp, print it as a code, drop it in a group. Guests need no account.

  3. 03

    Guests answer

    Yes or no, how many are coming, and an expected arrival time on a simple clock.

  4. 04

    Watch it form

    Confirmed, declined and waiting, plus plates to prepare and when the crowd actually lands.
