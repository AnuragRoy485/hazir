Why Hazir exists

Every Indian gathering runs on one unanswered question.

We use an app for rides, an app for food, an app for money. Yet when we invite people to the most important days of our lives, we still ask on WhatsApp, count replies by scrolling, and hope.

"Will try." "Maybe." A thumbs-up that means nothing. Then the real number arrives at the gate: forty fewer, or sixty more, and half of them two hours late. Food is wasted or runs out. Tables are wrong. A host who should be enjoying the day is on the phone.

The usual fixes do not help. Spreadsheets break at fifty rows. Group chats bury answers. Generic form tools ask the wrong questions and do not know that a family of four is one answer with four plates and four different meals.

Hazir asks three questions, the ones a host actually needs: are you coming, how many of you, and when will you arrive. The answers become a live headcount, a plate count and an arrival curve, the moment each guest taps send.

How it works

Four steps. No spreadsheet.

  1. 01

    Create the event

    Name it, set the date and the number you invited. Choose the occasion and get one link.

  2. 02

    Share one link

    Send it on WhatsApp, print it as a code, drop it in a group. Guests need no account.

  3. 03

    Guests answer

    Yes or no, how many are coming, and an expected arrival time on a simple clock.

  4. 04

    Watch it form

    Confirmed, declined and waiting, plus plates to prepare and when the crowd actually lands.
